Performance & Specs

The Veyron is built to push the limits of what a production car can do. Its engineering focuses on extreme speed, precise handling, and incredible acceleration. Every component, from the brakes to the tires and aerodynamics, is designed to perform perfectly under the most demanding conditions. The result is a supercar that delivers record-breaking performance while maintaining control and stability.

  • Top speed: 407 km/h (electronically limited)
  • 0–100 km/h (0–62 mph): 2.5 seconds
  • 0–200 km/h (0–124 mph): 7.3 seconds
  • Brakes: Carbon-ceramic discs for high-speed stopping power
  • Aerodynamics: Active rear wing and body adjustments for stability at high speeds
  • Drivetrain: All-wheel drive to manage extreme power safely
  • Tires: Special Michelin Pilot Sport PAX for grip at extreme speeds
